Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding trusted local help or offering your services on Handy Man Cameroon.

Handy Man Cameroon is a free online marketplace that connects people who need services (clients) with skilled local workers such as plumbers, electricians, cleaners, carpenters, nannies, painters, masons and many more across Cameroon.
Yes. Browsing workers, posting jobs, creating a worker profile and contacting workers is completely free. There are no hidden fees on the platform.
We support workers and jobs in Buea, Limbe, Douala, Yaoundé, Bamenda, Bafoussam, Kribi, Kumba, Garoua, Maroua, Ngaoundéré, Bertoua, Ebolowa, Dschang, Nkongsamba, Edéa, Mutengene, Tiko and many other towns across Cameroon. You can also enter a custom town.
Go to the Find Workers page. You can filter by category or town, or type a skill or name in the search box. Click any worker card to see their full profile, portfolio photos, ratings and contact buttons (WhatsApp or Call).
Create a free account on the Login / Register page, then go to Post a Job. Fill in the job title, category, description, town and optional budget. Workers in that category will be notified automatically.
Register a free account and choose “Offering services (Worker)”, or log in and visit Join as Worker. Add your town, main skill/category, description of services, years of experience and optional profile + portfolio photos. Your profile will appear on the Find Workers page so clients can contact you.
Handy Man Cameroon does not process or hold any payments. Clients and workers agree on the price and payment method directly (cash, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, etc.). Always discuss and agree on the price clearly before the work starts.
We strongly recommend checking ratings and reviews before hiring or accepting work. Meet in public places when possible, verify identity, and never send money in advance to someone you have not met. Use the Report button on any profile or job if you experience a problem.
Yes. Handy Man Cameroon supports both English and French. Many pages detect your browser language or provide a language switcher.
On every worker profile and job page you will find a “Report” button. You can also send us a message through the Contact Us page. Our team reviews every report as quickly as possible.
